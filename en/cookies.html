What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device, through the browser, to recognise you on later visits or to record your activity.

Cookies on this site

This site does not use cookies, its own or third-party, or any other tracking technologies. It has no visitor analytics, advertising or content embedded from other services. The fonts and images are served by the site itself.

For that reason, you are not asked for cookie consent.

Links to other sites

The links to Facebook and Google Maps only open those services if you click them. From then on, each service's own cookie policy applies.

Changes

If the site starts using cookies, this page will be updated and, where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before they are stored.
